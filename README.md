# Business Insight 360 Power BI Dashboard

## Overview

The **Business Insight 360 Power BI Dashboard** is an advanced business intelligence tool designed to provide a comprehensive view of key performance indicators (KPIs) across multiple business functions, including **Finance**, **Sales**, **Marketing**, **Supply Chain**, and **Executive** levels. The dashboard aggregates data from various business departments to deliver meaningful insights that support strategic decision-making.

This project utilizes **Power BI** to create interactive and dynamic reports that allow business users to track performance over time, compare against benchmarks, and make data-driven decisions.

## Key Features

### 1. **Integrated Business Views**
The dashboard integrates data from different business units, such as **Finance**, **Sales**, **Marketing**, **Supply Chain**, and **Executive Views**. This holistic approach allows stakeholders to monitor and evaluate performance from a top-down perspective, enabling insights across departments.

- **Finance View**: Focuses on financial performance, tracking Net Sales, Gross Margin, and profitability.
- **Sales View**: Provides insights into revenue, profit & loss statements, and performance over time.
- **Marketing View**: Monitors forecast accuracy, marketing effectiveness, and key performance metrics for product campaigns.
- **Supply Chain View**: Displays top-level insights related to the supply chain, providing information on product availability, lead times, and logistics.
- **Executive View**: Consolidates the most critical metrics from all areas for top executives to make high-level strategic decisions.

### 2. **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) & Benchmarking**
The dashboard is designed to track critical KPIs such as:

- **Net Sales**: The total revenue generated by the company from sales.
- **Gross Margin**: The difference between total revenue and the cost of goods sold (COGS).
- **Net Profit**: The remaining profit after all operational expenses are deducted from gross margin.

These KPIs are benchmarked against **historical performance** and **target goals**, offering users insight into whether the business is on track to meet its objectives. The dashboard also supports **year-over-year comparisons** and **region-wise performance breakdowns**.

### 3. **Forecast Accuracy Monitoring**
This feature allows for the tracking of **forecast accuracy** across different metrics, such as sales, profit, and market performance. By comparing **forecasted** versus **actual** values, the dashboard identifies areas of discrepancies and highlights trends in prediction errors.

The tool includes:

- **Net Error**: The difference between forecasted and actual values.
- **Risk Profiling**: Identifies areas where forecasts are less reliable, helping stakeholders make more informed decisions about potential risks.

### 4. **Interactive Slicers**
The dashboard leverages **interactive slicers** to provide users with the ability to filter and view data based on various dimensions such as **region**, **product**, **customer**, and **time period**. 

- **Region Slicer**: Allows users to view data for specific geographical regions (e.g., North America, APAC, Europe).
- **Product Slicer**: Filters data by product categories, showing how specific products are performing.
- **Customer Slicer**: Displays insights based on customer segmentation.
- **Time Period Slicer**: Allows users to track performance over different time frames, such as monthly, quarterly, or yearly.

These slicers dynamically update the visualizations and KPIs, providing a tailored view of the data that adapts to user needs.

### 5. **Data Context Awareness**
With slicers and filters in place, the dashboard ensures **context awareness**. When users select different slicer options, all relevant metrics and visuals automatically update to reflect the new context. For example, selecting a specific region or product category will instantly update KPIs such as **Net Sales**, **Gross Margin**, and **Net Profit** to match the selected criteria.

This interactive feature provides users with a seamless experience and allows them to drill down into data without losing sight of the overall performance.

## Tools Used

- **Power BI Desktop**: Used for creating the interactive dashboard and visualizations.
- **DAX**: Applied for creating calculated columns and measures that drive KPIs and dynamic insights.
- **Power Query**: Used for data transformation, cleaning, and integration.
- **Excel** (optional): For exporting data and running additional analysis if necessary.
